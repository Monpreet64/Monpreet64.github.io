B.E. Capstone Project
Assam Engineering College, Guwahati Final Year Project

Design and Fatigue Failure Analysis of a Leaf Spring using ANSYS

Abstract & Methodology: Automotive multi-leaf springs are critical suspension components subjected to continuous, cyclic, and stochastic impact loads transmitted from uneven road surfaces. This research focused on comprehensive computer-aided design (CAD) and finite element analysis (FEA) to evaluate the structural integrity, stress distribution, and fatigue failure life of a semi-elliptic leaf spring assembly.

A 3D parametric CAD model was constructed based on standard commercial vehicle specifications. Static structural and fatigue analyses were conducted within ANSYS Workbench to analyze von Mises stress contours, maximum deflection under peak payload, and localized stress concentrations near the master leaf eye ends and center clamp interface.

Fatigue life and damage accumulation were evaluated using the ANSYS Fatigue Tool utilizing the stress-life (S-N) approach with Goodman mean stress correction. The study identified critical cyclic failure locations and provided insights into optimal thickness gradations and material selections to enhance fatigue endurance and operational safety.

Software Tools: ANSYS Static Structural, ANSYS Fatigue Tool, SolidWorks, AutoCAD
Key Analytical Focus: Von Mises Stress, Deflection, S-N Fatigue Life, Goodman Mean Stress Correction
ANSYS FEA Fatigue Failure Analysis Structural Mechanics CAD Modeling SolidWorks Mechanical Engineering

Assam Engineering College / Core Algorithms Computational Algorithms

Function Optimization via Direct and Gradient-Based Search Methods

Abstract & Methodology: Engineered numerical optimization routines from scratch in pure C language to eliminate third-party library overhead and evaluate algorithm efficiency for non-linear single-variable and multi-variable benchmark functions.

Implemented the Bounding Phase Method as a direct, derivative-free search technique to reliably establish an initial unimodal bracket containing the global or local optimum through exponential step-size scaling. Integrated the Bisection Method as a first-order gradient-based approach to iteratively halve the search interval until convergence within tight numerical tolerance.

Implementation: Pure C Language (Standard C99, Memory-Safe Pointers)
Techniques: Bounding Phase Method, Bisection Method, Unimodal Bracketing
